# Returns and Right of Withdrawal

This page provides information about the consumer’s right to withdraw from a distance contract, about returning goods, and about the procedure for complaints or damage to goods.

## 1. Right of withdrawal

For a distance contract, the consumer has the right to withdraw from the contract without giving a reason within the statutory period, generally within 14 days of taking delivery of the goods, unless a statutory exception applies to the particular case.

Withdrawal from the contract must be notified to the seller by an unequivocal statement of the decision to withdraw from the contract.

For this purpose you may use the form available on this page or contact us using the details provided below.

If you submit the notice electronically through our form, we will confirm receipt by email.

## 3. Refund of payments

After a valid withdrawal from the contract, we will refund to the consumer the payments we received from the consumer in connection with the relevant part of the contract, to the extent and within the time limit set by applicable law.

If the consumer withdraws from the entire contract, the refund of payments also includes the costs of the least expensive standard delivery method we offered at the time of the order, where required by applicable law.

We will generally refund the payment using the same method the consumer used for the original payment, unless we expressly agree with the consumer on another method that does not cause the consumer further costs.

Where goods are returned in connection with withdrawal, the seller may, in cases permitted by law, withhold the refund until the goods have been received back or the consumer has provided evidence of having sent them, whichever occurs first.

Internal processing of the refund may be carried out manually according to the original payment method. This does not affect the consumer’s statutory rights or the statutory time limits for refunds.

## 4. Partial withdrawal

If the withdrawal concerns only part of an order, the return of goods and the related payments are assessed in relation to that part of the order.

The amount of any refund depends on the scope of the withdrawal, the value of the returned goods, any discounts, shipping costs and other circumstances under applicable law.

## 5. Live plants and statutory exceptions

For live plants, their natural biological character and condition at the time of return must be taken into account.

The right of withdrawal applies to the extent set by the applicable legal provisions. For goods that are liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly, or for other goods covered by a statutory exception, the right of withdrawal may be limited or excluded.

The consumer is liable for any diminished value of the goods resulting from handling beyond what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of the goods, to the extent set by applicable law.

For live plants, this may include, for example, damage caused by unsuitable storage, insufficient or excessive watering, frost, overheating, improper handling or other unsuitable conditions after delivery.

## 6. Damage in transit and complaints

If goods were delivered damaged, incomplete or not as ordered, this is generally a matter of liability for defects, not withdrawal from the contract without giving a reason.

We recommend checking the shipment upon receipt and documenting any visible damage.

If you discover damage, a defect or a mismatch with the order, contact us as soon as possible and, where possible, include the order number and photographs.

This recommendation does not limit the consumer’s statutory rights.

More detailed information on complaints is set out in the [[terms|General Terms and Conditions]].

## 7. Confirmation of receipt of the notice

If you submit a withdrawal notice via the electronic form, we will send you confirmation of receipt to the email address stated in the form.

The confirmation includes in particular a reference number, the date and time of receipt, and details needed to identify the relevant order.

Confirmation of receipt of the notice alone does not mean confirmation that returned goods have been received or that a refund has been made.
